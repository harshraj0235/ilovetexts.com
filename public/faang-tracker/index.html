    ⭐ STAR Method Framework

    S

    Situation

    Set the context briefly. Keep it to 20-30 seconds. Only provide details that are necessary.

    T

    Task

    What was your specific responsibility? What was the challenge or goal?

    A

    Action

    This is 50% of your answer. Detail what YOU specifically did. Use "I", not "we".

    R

    Result

    Share quantifiable outcomes. "Reduced latency by 20%", "Saved $50k annually".

    📝 Your STAR Story Bank

    Prepare 8-10 versatile stories. Each story can be adapted for multiple question types.
