Map & scenery coverage

Road paths and map positions come from OpenStreetMap routing. 3D roads are simplified and flat. Mapped buildings, rivers, bridges and vegetation load near your car when available. Individual trees, terrain heights, road widths and building appearances are not a complete real-world reconstruction. Route assist follows the road; you control speed and lane position. Traffic is simulated.

Online services can be unavailable. A dated Delhi–Rishikesh route snapshot is included; other routes need a connection. Some islands and disconnected roads cannot be joined by car.
